From Mentana you can reach many destinations in a day. Ten ideas, from the closest to the farthest.

1. **Tivoli**: Villa d'Este and Hadrian's Villa, two UNESCO villas.
2. **Castelli Romani**: Frascati, Castel Gandolfo and Lake Nemi.
3. **Lake Bracciano**: Anguillara and Trevignano Romano.
4. **Cerveteri and Tarquinia**: the Etruscan necropolises.
5. **Viterbo**: medieval centre and free hot springs.
6. **Bomarzo**: the Park of the Monsters, much loved by children.
7. **Civita di Bagnoregio**: the suspended village.
8. **Orvieto**: the Duomo and the city on the tufa.
9. **Sabaudia and Circeo**: dunes, sea and national park.
10. **Pompeii**: a long day, but worth it.
