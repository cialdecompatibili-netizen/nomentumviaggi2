A coach costs the same with 10 people or with 40. To keep the price low, each tour has a minimum number of participants, usually about 20.

## What happens

- You pre-register without paying.
- Once the minimum is reached, the tour is confirmed.
- At least 7 days before, we tell you the date, the times and the pick-up points.
- If the minimum is not reached, the tour does not go ahead and you pay nothing.

All the details are on the [How it works]({% include url-ref.html ref="come-funziona" %}) page.
