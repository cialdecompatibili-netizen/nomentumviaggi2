Anyone living in Mentana and the surrounding area has snow less than two hours away by road. These are the most popular destinations for a day.

## Monte Livata

It is the mountain closest to Rome: about an hour and a half of travel. Easy slopes, suitable for families and beginners.

## Terminillo

In the Reatini Mountains, about two hours away. Larger and with a greater drop in height: good for skiing but also for snowshoeing and walks.

## Campo Staffi and Campo Catino

Small resorts in southern Lazio, convenient for a quiet day.

## Before you leave

- Always check the resorts' websites: openings and ski passes change every season.
- If there is no snow or the roads are unsafe, the trip is moved.
- By coach there are no snow chains to fit and no parking to look for.
