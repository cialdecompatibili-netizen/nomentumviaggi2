Nomentum Viaggi was born in **Mentana**, ancient *Nomentum*, about 25 km from Rome. It is a convenient position: in a short time you can reach Rome, the villages of the Tuscia, the Castelli Romani, the mountains and the sea.

We organise day trips, weekends, days in the snow, wine tours, evenings in Rome and trips for groups, schools and companies, with guides, drivers and partner facilities.

## How we work

- **Pre-registration without payment**: we set off when the group is ready.
- **Departures close to home**: pick-up points are chosen among the participants.
- **Clear programmes**: each page says what is included and what is not.
