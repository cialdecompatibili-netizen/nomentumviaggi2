We organise group trips and tours. To keep prices low, a coach only leaves when there are enough people: that is why we work with **pre-registration**.

## The steps

1. **Choose a tour** and write to us on WhatsApp or through the form.
2. **Pre-register without paying.** We add your name to the list for that date.
3. **We reach the minimum** (usually about 20 people). We let you know as soon as the tour is confirmed.
4. **At least 7 days before** we tell you the date, the times and 2-3 convenient pick-up points for the participants.
5. **We set off.** If the minimum is not reached, the tour does not go ahead and you pay nothing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I get on the coach?** Pick-up points are chosen according to the participants: Mentana, Monterotondo, Fonte Nuova, Sant'Angelo Romano and Rome.

**What is included?** Each page says what is included (coach, guide, hotel) and what is not (entrance fees, meals). The prices shown are starting prices.

**Can I book my own group?** Yes: [groups, schools and companies]({% include url-ref.html ref="gruppi-e-aziende" %}) have a dedicated quote.
